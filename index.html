How grading works

Running out of tries, or pressing "I don't know", counts as Again. After a correct click you grade yourself: Again if it was a lucky guess, Hard if you hesitated, Good if you knew it, Easy if it was instant. New countries come back after 1 and 10 minutes before they graduate to a 1-day interval.

When you click one country in place of another, that pair is added to your mix-ups in Progress, and the country you clicked by mistake comes up for review within a day so the two get compared again soon.

After an answer you can zoom around and tap countries to see their names. Any country you look up this way sits out for 10 minutes so it isn't a free point. If it has to come up sooner and you get it right, it doesn't count and comes back once the 10 minutes are up.

Keys: 1 Again, 2 Hard, 3 Good, 4 Easy. Space or Enter picks the highlighted grade, or Continue.
